The Merrell Moab 3 Shoes are rugged, all-terrain hiking shoes designed for comfort, durability, and superior traction on any trail. Featuring a breathable mesh and suede leather upper with a protective rubber toe cap, they offer ventilation and durability in rough conditions. The Merrell Air Cushion technology provides excellent shock absorption and stability, while the Kinetic Fit™ Advanced insole and lightweight EVA foam midsole ensure long-lasting comfort. With a Vibram® TC5+ outsole for exceptional grip on wet and dry surfaces and sustainable materials like 100% recycled laces, webbing, and mesh lining, the Moab 3 is the perfect choice for hikers seeking reliability, performance, and eco-conscious design.
